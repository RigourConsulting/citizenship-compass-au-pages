Citizenship Compass AU Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026
Last updated: 4 October 2026

Privacy summary

Citizenship Compass AU is designed by Rigour Consulting to keep your learning progress, journey notes and profile information on your own device. We do not require an online account, do not collect personal identifiers, and do not include third-party advertising or behavioural analytics libraries.

Local processing versus developer collection

Under modern digital privacy standards, including Google Play Data safety guidelines, personal data is defined as collected when it is transmitted off the user's device to the developer or an external party.

Citizenship Compass AU is built around local-first processing:

  • Local processing. Your question responses, study statistics, optional profile preferences, and residence guide dates are processed and stored solely on your device. They are not transmitted to Rigour Consulting.
  • Developer collection. Rigour Consulting does not operate an account database or analytics telemetry backend for Citizenship Compass AU. We do not collect or receive your personal study history, profile information, or travel dates.

The Android application stores the following information locally in protected application storage:

Information never requested or collected

The application is strictly a study aid. It is deliberately isolated from official government record systems. The application does not request, collect, or store:

  • Legal full name as a mandatory requirement
  • Residential street address
  • Passport numbers, expiry dates, or passport photo scans
  • Visa grant notice numbers, visa grant letters, or visa labels
  • Identity document uploads, driver licences, or national identity cards
  • Department of Home Affairs ImmiAccount usernames, passwords, or login tokens
  • GPS location, precise location, or approximate device location (any city or region in My Compass is voluntarily typed by the user and is never queried from device location sensors)
  • Device contact lists, call logs, SMS messages, or calendar events
  • Advertising identifiers (GAID / IDFA) or cross-app tracking identifiers
  • Third-party analytics, behavioral event tracking, or session recording SDKs

Profile photographs and media access

If you choose to set an avatar image for your optional My Compass profile:

  • System Photo Picker. The Android application uses the modern Android system Photo Picker. The application does not request or require broad read access to your entire photo library or shared device storage.
  • Private local storage. The selected photo is copied directly into internal application-private storage on your device. It is used exclusively to display your avatar within the app.
  • No server transmission. Your photo is never transmitted to Rigour Consulting or any remote server.
  • Easy removal. You can remove your avatar image at any time in Settings. Removing the photo permanently deletes the image file from internal application storage.

Backup and device transfer rules

To safeguard your personal information against unintended cloud exposure or device cloning, the current Android implementation configures explicit backup rules:

  • Cloud backup exclusion. The local My Compass profile avatar directory and user preferences DataStore are explicitly excluded from Android Auto Backup. They are not uploaded to Google Drive or Android cloud backup services.
  • Device transfer exclusion. The profile avatar is also excluded from direct device-to-device migration transfers.
  • Practical outcome. If you migrate to a new device or restore from a cloud backup, your profile avatar and local preferences will not automatically carry over. You can re-enter your preferred name or select a fresh photo on your new phone.

Data export transparency

Normal users using the production release cannot export study progress and configuration settings to external JSON files. This ensures unencrypted study history and travel dates are not inadvertently saved to shared device storage.

Internal psychometric research export tooling is restricted exclusively to debug development builds (`BuildConfig.DEBUG`) and is completely removed by compiler minification from release builds distributed on app stores.

Network activity and data leaving the device

Citizenship Compass AU is designed to function offline. Outbound network communication is limited to the following explicit actions:

1. Official Home Affairs audio streaming

The app allows you to optionally stream official Our Common Bond podcast episodes. Audio playback is initiated solely by your explicit tap on the play button. When streaming, your device connects directly to the official media content delivery network (`mcdn.podbean.com`) linked by the Department of Home Affairs. The media server receives standard technical metadata required for internet streaming (such as your device IP address and standard HTTP GET headers). The app attaches zero user identifiers, zero study progress records, and zero profile details to the request.

2. External official links

Tapping links to official Department of Home Affairs publications, video resources, or statutory legislation launches your device's external web browser. These websites operate under their own independent privacy notices and terms.

3. Commercial in-app purchases

In-app purchase transactions are handled securely by Google Play Billing on Android. Payment transactions occur through Google Play Services under Google's Privacy Policy. Rigour Consulting does not receive, process, or store your credit card or financial account numbers. Real paid purchases remain safely disabled in current builds pending the deployment of a cryptographic server-side purchase verification service.

Data retention, control and deletion

Because your study data is stored on your device, you have immediate, direct control over its retention and deletion without needing to contact support:

  • Reset study progress and bookmarks. In the Settings screen, tap Reset learning progress to permanently wipe all recorded question attempts, mastery stages, review queues, and custom bookmarks.
  • Reset citizenship journey. Clear your selected milestone stage and reset the journey roadmap to its initial state.
  • Reset residence guide dates. Clear all entered travel dates and permanent residency dates from the indicative residence guide.
  • Remove profile information. Clear your preferred name, state or territory, or city at any time, or tap Remove photo to permanently delete the avatar file.
  • Complete local data reset. Tap Reset all local data in Settings to instantly restore the entire application to its original installation state.
  • Device uninstallation. Uninstalling the application from your device permanently removes all internal databases, shared preferences, and private files.

Because there are no remote user accounts, there is no online account to delete.

Platform-specific disclosures

Children and young learners

Citizenship Compass AU is designed for adult and family learners preparing for Australian citizenship. The application does not collect personal information from children under the age of 13 or any other age group.

Changes to this privacy policy

If our privacy practices or technical architecture change, we will update this page with a revised effective date. Any future capability involving remote data transmission will require explicit, informed consent.
