Privacy policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

Private by default

Citizenship Compass AU is designed so that your personal learning data stays on your device. We do not require an account, we do not track you across apps, and we do not run third-party advertising or analytics services.

Our approach to personal privacy

Preparing for Australian citizenship involves personal commitment, study time, and sometimes sensitive travel or residence dates. We believe your study progress belongs exclusively to you. Rigour Consulting designed Citizenship Compass AU to function completely offline without creating accounts or transmitting study history to remote servers.

Information stored on your device

The application stores the following information locally in protected Android application storage:

  • Practice and mock test attempts. Records of questions answered, choices selected, response times, and whether the answer was correct, used to power your study statistics and review scheduling.
  • Spaced review schedules. Timestamps indicating when specific questions or syllabus objectives are next due for review.
  • Syllabus progress. Lessons marked as completed and your demonstrated mastery across curriculum sections.
  • Optional mistake reflections. Personal tags you select after an incorrect answer (such as noting a rushed answer or mixed-up concept) to help tailor your revision.
  • Indicative residence calculator records. Any travel dates or Australian permanent residency dates you choose to enter into the planning calculator. This information never leaves your device.
  • Application preferences. Your chosen display settings, text-to-speech preferences, and notification times.

Data not collected

We do not collect or transmit:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or government identity documents.
  • Location information or GPS coordinates.
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking identifiers.
  • Audio recordings from text-to-speech features (which run through Android built-in speech synthesis).
  • Remote analytics, telemetry, or behavioral tracking events.

In-app purchases and Google Play

Citizenship Compass AU offers an optional one-time purchase called Complete Compass, processed securely through the Google Play Store. When you make a purchase, Google handles payment processing. Citizenship Compass AU receives only an encrypted digital receipt token from Google Play to verify that your purchase is active on your device. We do not receive or store your credit card or financial details.

Data retention and how to delete your data

Because all learning information is stored directly on your device, you have complete control over its retention:

  • In-app reset. You can clear your question attempts, review queue, and study history at any time by opening Settings and selecting Reset learning progress.
  • Android system clearing. You can delete all application storage by opening your device Settings > Apps > Citizenship Compass AU > Storage > Clear data.
  • App uninstallation. Removing the application from your device permanently deletes all local databases and stored preferences.

Children and young learners

Citizenship Compass AU is designed for adult and family learners preparing for Australian citizenship. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 or any other age group.

Changes to this privacy policy

If we ever update our technical architecture or add new optional capabilities, we will revise this policy and update the date at the top of this page. Any future features involving remote data collection will require explicit opt-in consent.
