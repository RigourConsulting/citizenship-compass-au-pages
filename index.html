Citizenship Compass AU

Know what matters. Learn with purpose. Be ready.

Citizenship Compass AU helps aspiring citizens prepare thoroughly for the Australian citizenship test. Built directly from the official testable public resource, Australian Citizenship: Our Common Bond, the application combines structured learning with spaced repetition, realistic practice mock tests, journey guidance and complete local privacy.

The Android application is the current implementation. An iOS version is planned. Platform-specific information will be added before release.

What Citizenship Compass AU helps with

Preparing for citizenship involves understanding Australian democracy, history, legal structures and community values. Citizenship Compass AU transforms this material into a calm, adult learning experience that respects your time and your privacy.

Learn the official curriculum

All learning content aligns with the testable parts of Australian Citizenship: Our Common Bond, published by the Australian Government Department of Home Affairs.

Part 1

Australia and its people

Explore Australia's ancient First Nations heritage, British settlement, democratic development, cultural diversity, and national symbols including flags, the coat of arms and the national anthem.

Part 2

Democratic beliefs, rights and liberties

Understand parliamentary democracy, the rule of law, freedom of speech, freedom of association, freedom of religion, equality under the law, and equality of opportunity.

Part 3

Government and the law in Australia

Study the Australian Constitution, the roles of the Governor-General, Prime Minister and Parliament, the three spheres of government, how laws are made, and the role of courts and police.

Part 4

Australian values

Master the dedicated testable values section covering mutual respect, individual freedom, active community contribution, and integration into the Australian society.

Practise under real conditions

The practice engine reinforces long-term retention rather than temporary memorisation.

  • Question bank breadth. 240 practice questions evaluate knowledge across 120 distinct item families, ensuring you experience varied stems and options.
  • Australian values drills. Because official test rules require applicants to correctly answer all five Australian values questions, dedicated values practice helps you build certainty on this pass-critical section.
  • Realistic practice mock tests. Simulated tests draw 20 random questions under a 45-minute timer without revealing answer keys prematurely, mirroring official test centre conditions.
  • Metacognitive reflection. After answering, review clear pedagogical rationales and reflect on mistake patterns (such as misreading a question or confusing similar dates) to focus future study.

Understand your progress

Retained

Verified long-term factual recall across multiple spaced review intervals.

Follow your citizenship journey

Preparing for citizenship involves practical administrative milestones alongside study. The application supports your broader pathway:

  • Milestone roadmap. Track eight key stages from initial eligibility assessment, application lodgement and appointment notification, through to test day, ceremony invitation and taking the Australian Citizenship Pledge.
  • Indicative general residence guide. Check your potential qualification under the standard four-year lawful residence requirement under section 22 of the Australian Citizenship Act 2007. Enter your presence and absence dates to estimate compliance with the four-year presence rule and absence limits. This guide provides planning estimates and does not determine formal eligibility.
  • Official service links. Access direct links to official Department of Home Affairs portals, including ImmiAccount and official testing appointment guides.

Privacy by design

We believe your personal study journey and travel history belong exclusively to you. Citizenship Compass AU operates on a local-first privacy foundation:

Local device data boundary

Everything related to your preparation is stored locally in private application storage on your own device:

My Compass profile

Your optional preferred name, state or territory, and local avatar photograph remain entirely on your device. No legal name or identity document is required.

Zero tracking or ads

No advertising SDKs, no behavioral analytics, no third-party tracking libraries, and no background location queries.

No cloud account

No user account creation is required. You can study completely offline without signing in or providing an email address.

Complete local reset

You can inspect your data in the app and permanently clear your study history, journey milestones, or all local data at any time in Settings.
