A calm and structured companion for the Australian citizenship test

Citizenship Compass AU helps you prepare thoroughly for the Australian citizenship test. Built around the official testable resource, Australian Citizenship: Our Common Bond, the app combines structured learning with spaced repetition, realistic practice tests and local privacy.

Study with clarity and confidence

Curriculum

Complete syllabus coverage

Explore all four syllabus parts in manageable lessons: Australia and its people, Democratic beliefs, rights and liberties, Government and the law in Australia, and Australian values.

Practice

Realistic mock tests

Practise with authentic 20-question mock tests under a 45-minute continuous clock. The app evaluates both criteria required for passing: at least 75 percent overall (15/20) and 100 percent on Australian values questions (5/5).

Memory

Spaced review schedule

Reinforce what you learn over time. Our spaced review system brings back questions at helpful intervals so that key facts remain fresh in your mind until test day.

Rhythm

Calm study rhythm

Track your study activity over recent days without anxiety. There are no punitive streaks, no guilt notifications, and no artificial readiness percentages.

Privacy

Private by design

Your learning history, practice attempts and study notes remain on your device in secure storage. The app includes no advertising, no tracking software, and no remote telemetry.

Inclusion

Accessible and readable

Designed to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards with high-contrast colour themes, full screen reader support through Android TalkBack, large typography scaling, and reduced motion options.

Clear and honest pricing
