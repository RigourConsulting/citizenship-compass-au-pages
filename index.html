Independent study resource

Citizenship Compass AU is an independent educational app created by Rigour Consulting. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Australian Government or the Department of Home Affairs.

A calm and structured companion for the Australian citizenship test

Citizenship Compass AU helps you prepare thoroughly for the Australian citizenship test. Built around the official testable resource, Australian Citizenship: Our Common Bond, the app combines structured learning with spaced repetition, realistic practice mock tests and complete local privacy.

Lessons, practice, mock tests, Journey milestones and saved progress work offline. Optional official audio and external government links require an internet connection.

Study with clarity and confidence

Curriculum

Complete syllabus coverage

Explore all 65 lessons across the four syllabus parts: Australia and its people, Democratic beliefs, rights and liberties, Government and the law in Australia, and Australian values.

Practice

Realistic practice mock tests

Practise with realistic 20-question mock tests under a 45-minute continuous clock. The app evaluates both criteria required for passing: at least 75 percent overall (15 out of 20) and 100 percent on Australian values questions (5 out of 5).

Memory

Spaced review schedule

Reinforce what you learn over time. Our spaced review system brings back questions at helpful intervals so that key facts remain fresh in your mind until test day.

Rhythm

Calm study rhythm

Track your study activity over recent days without anxiety. There are no punitive streaks, no guilt notifications, and no artificial readiness percentages.

Privacy

Private by design

Your learning history, practice attempts and study notes remain on your device in secure local storage. The app includes no advertising, no tracking software, and no remote telemetry.

Inclusion

Accessible and readable

Citizenship Compass AU is designed and tested against relevant WCAG 2.2 Level AA accessibility criteria, including TalkBack screen reader support, dynamic font scaling up to 200 percent, high contrast themes, and reduced motion options.
