Frequently asked questions

How does the mock test match the official test format

Our realistic mock tests follow the official examination structure published by the Department of Home Affairs:

  • 20 multiple choice questions selected randomly from across the testable syllabus.
  • Exactly 5 questions addressing Part 4 (Australian values).
  • A continuous 45-minute countdown clock that matches the official test environment.
  • Dual-standard passing evaluation: you must score at least 75 percent overall (15 out of 20) and correctly answer all 5 Australian values questions (5 out of 5).

Can I study offline without an internet connection

Yes. All syllabus text, practice questions, civics glossaries, and practice analytics are stored directly on your phone. You can study on public transport, in flight, or in areas with limited mobile reception without interruption.

How does the spaced review schedule work

When you answer questions during practice sessions, the app tracks how confidently and accurately you recall the material. Questions you find challenging are scheduled for earlier review, while questions you answer correctly are reviewed at gradually increasing intervals. You can see your upcoming review load for the next seven days on your progress screen.

Why are Australian values questions treated differently

Under official Australian citizenship test rules introduced in November 2020, applicants must correctly answer every Australian values question to pass the test. Even if you score 19 out of 20 overall, missing a single values question results in a test fail. Citizenship Compass AU includes dedicated values drills to ensure you are thoroughly prepared for this requirement.

How does the indicative residence calculator work

The journey section includes an indicative calculator based on the standard general residence requirement under section 22 of the Australian Citizenship Act 2007. It calculates whether you meet the four-year physical presence rule, the maximum 12 months absence limit across four years, and the maximum 90 days absence limit in the past 12 months as a permanent resident. This calculator provides planning estimates only and does not replace official eligibility assessments by the Department of Home Affairs.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone

If you upgrade or replace your Android phone, make sure you are signed in to the Google Play Store with the same Google account used for your original purchase. Open Citizenship Compass AU, go to Settings, and select Restore purchase. The app will immediately verify your receipt with Google Play and restore full access.

How do I reset my progress or clear all data

To start your study journey fresh, navigate to Settings and tap Reset learning progress. This action permanently clears all recorded question attempts, review queues, and mastery statistics on your device.
