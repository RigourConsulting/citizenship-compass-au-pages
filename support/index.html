Getting started with your study

Citizenship Compass AU is structured to guide your revision systematically:

  • Onboarding. Choose your current preparation milestone (such as initial research, application lodged, or test booked) and optionally set a target test date countdown and daily study reminder.
  • Learn tab. Read the 65 curriculum lessons organised across the four official syllabus parts from Our Common Bond.
  • Practice hub. Build factual recall through quick drills, dedicated Australian Values practice, and full realistic practice mock tests.
  • Progress screen. Review your demonstrated construct mastery across all 65 curriculum objectives and check your upcoming spaced review queue.

Frequently asked questions

How do realistic practice mock tests work

Our practice mock tests mirror the official examination structure administered by the Department of Home Affairs:

  • 20 multiple-choice questions drawn randomly across the testable syllabus.
  • Exactly 5 questions addressing Part 4 (Australian values).
  • A continuous 45-minute countdown clock matching test centre conditions.
  • Dual-standard pass evaluation: you must achieve at least 75 percent overall (15 out of 20) and correctly answer all 5 Australian values questions (5 out of 5).
  • To reflect genuine exam conditions, the live test session does not show whether a question is a values question while answering. Full breakdowns appear on the recap screen upon completion.

Can I use the app completely offline

Yes. All 65 curriculum lessons, 240 practice questions, realistic mock tests, Journey milestones, residence guide calculations, and saved study progress work entirely offline without an internet connection.

An internet connection is required only if you choose to stream official podcast episodes or tap external links to official government websites.

How do I personalise My Compass and manage my profile

My Compass personalisation is completely optional. You can enter a preferred name, select an Australian State or Territory, and choose an optional profile avatar photograph:

  • Photo selection. Tapping the avatar launches the privacy-preserving Android system Photo Picker. You do not need to grant broad storage permissions.
  • Photo storage. The chosen image is copied to private application storage on your device and is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Photo removal. You can tap Remove photo in Settings to delete the image file from private storage immediately.
  • No legal name. You do not need to provide your legal name or identity documents.

How do theme options and Dynamic Colour work

In Settings, you can configure your preferred appearance:

  • System Default. Matches your device's light or dark mode schedule.
  • Force Light. Keeps the application in light mode regardless of device settings.
  • Force Dark. Displays a high-contrast true black AMOLED background designed to reduce eye strain and conserve battery life.
  • Dynamic Colour. On compatible Android devices, you can toggle Dynamic Colour to harmonise the application palette with your device wallpaper accents.

How does text to speech work

During practice sessions, you can tap the speaker icon to listen to question prompts and answer choices read aloud using your device's built-in Android text-to-speech engine. Audio playback can be paused or stopped at any point.

How does official Home Affairs audio streaming work

The app provides access to official Our Common Bond podcast episodes. Tapping play streams the audio over HTTPS from the official media host (`mcdn.podbean.com`). If you are offline, the app displays a clear notice and lets you continue reading the lesson or transcript text.

How does the indicative residence guide work

The residence guide calculates potential qualification under the standard four-year general residence requirement in section 22 of the Australian Citizenship Act 2007. It checks:

  • Four-year physical presence in Australia prior to the planned application date.
  • Maximum absence of 12 months (365 days) across the four-year period.
  • Permanent residency presence for at least 12 months immediately preceding the application.
  • Maximum absence of 90 days in the 12 months preceding the application.

This guide provides planning estimates and does not replace official advice from the Department of Home Affairs.

What is the status of Complete Compass purchases

Citizenship Compass AU includes an entitlement architecture for an optional one-time upgrade known as Complete Compass, which unlocks unlimited practice mock tests, the full spaced retrieval queue, and targeted weak area drills.

Current commercial status: The commercial user interface, billing client, and local entitlement storage are fully built. However, production payment processing remains safely disabled pending the deployment of a cryptographic server-side receipt verification service. Users cannot currently complete paid transactions within the app.

How will purchase restoration work when enabled

When production purchases are live, purchases will be tied to your Google Play account. On a new or replacement Android phone signed in to the same Google account, tapping Restore purchase in Settings will re-verify your entitlement through Google Play.

How do I reset my progress or delete local data

You can reset your data at any time directly in Settings:

  • Reset learning progress. Clears question attempts, mastery stages, and review queues while retaining lesson reading states.
  • Reset journey milestones. Clears milestone selections.
  • Reset residence guide. Clears entered presence and travel dates.
  • Reset all local data. Restores the application to its initial state.
