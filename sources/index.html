Government information and copyright attribution

Citizenship Compass AU references official educational materials published by the Commonwealth of Australia. All practice questions and pedagogical explanations are independently authored by Rigour Consulting.

Primary testable resource

The official testable resource for the Australian citizenship test is published by the Australian Government Department of Home Affairs under the title Australian Citizenship: Our Common Bond.

Our curriculum lessons and practice questions align with the four testable sections:

  • Part 1 Australia and its people. History, Indigenous peoples, geographical diversity, and national symbols including the flag, coat of arms, and national anthem.
  • Part 2 Australia's democratic beliefs, rights and liberties. Parliamentary democracy, rule of law, freedom of speech, freedom of association, freedom of religion, and equality of men and women.
  • Part 3 Government and the law in Australia. The Australian Constitution, roles of the Governor-General, Prime Minister, Parliament, judiciary, law-making process, and three tiers of government (federal, state and territory, local).
  • Part 4 Australian values. The dedicated testable section focusing on mutual respect, individual freedom, equality of opportunity, active contribution to society, and integration into the Australian community.

Official podcast audio and transcripts

The five episodes of the official Our Common Bond podcast series are streamed on demand from the official content delivery network host (mcdn.podbean.com) linked by the Department of Home Affairs. Audio files are not bundled or redistributed within the application package. Transcripts published on official Department pages are cited for learning reference and accessibility.

Statutory and legislative references

Our journey planning guides and residence calculator reference public legislation enacted by the Parliament of Australia:

  • Australian Citizenship Act 2007 (Cth), specifically section 21 (application and eligibility) and section 22 (general residence requirement).
  • Australian Citizenship Regulations 2016 (Cth).
  • Migration Act 1958 (Cth).

Commonwealth legislation is accessible to the public via the Australian Federal Register of Legislation at legislation.gov.au.
