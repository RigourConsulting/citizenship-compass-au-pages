Accessibility statement

Last updated 3 October 2026

Committed to accessible civics education

Citizenship Compass AU is designed and tested against relevant WCAG 2.2 Level AA accessibility criteria across our Android application and web documentation.

Core accessibility features

Screen reader support and TalkBack

Citizenship Compass AU has been built and tested with Android TalkBack. Interactive components provide meaningful accessibility descriptions, test questions announce current question index and total question count, and practice options declare their selected state clearly. Headings are structured semantically so you can navigate by heading with screen reader shortcuts.

Dynamic typography and text scaling

All screens support Android system font scaling up to 200 percent. Card layouts and scrolling containers expand flexibly to accommodate larger font sizes without truncating key information or clipping question choices.

High contrast and colour independence

Our visual design employs an authentic Australian palette designed for clear contrast. Standard text meets or exceeds the WCAG 4.5:1 contrast ratio against light and dark surfaces. Information is never conveyed through colour alone: answer feedback pairs distinct iconography (check marks, cross marks) and descriptive text labels with accent colours.

Touch targets and physical controls

All clickable buttons, list cards, and tab selectors maintain a minimum interactive touch target of 48 by 48 density-independent pixels (dp). Generous spacing between option cards prevents accidental taps.

Reduced motion and calm interactions

The app respects device-level reduced motion settings. Transition animations and decorative effects are disabled or simplified when reduced motion is enabled in system settings, preventing motion sickness or distraction.

Integrated audio and speech support

Learners can listen to lesson content and question text using integrated text-to-speech. Speech playback can be paused, resumed, or adjusted at any point during practice.
