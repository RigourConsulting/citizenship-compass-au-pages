Privacy choices and local data management

Citizenship Compass AU provides immediate, in-app controls so you can manage your personal preferences, clear study history, and configure application permissions without submitting forms or contacting support.

No online account exists

Citizenship Compass AU operates entirely on your device without user registration, usernames, passwords, or cloud accounts. Therefore, there is no online user account to delete.

Managing your My Compass profile

All profile personalisation in My Compass is optional. You can update or remove your details at any time in the app:

  • Preferred name. Edit or clear your preferred name at any time in Settings. If cleared, the app displays a neutral greeting.
  • State or Territory. Change your chosen Australian State or Territory or set it to unselected.
  • City or region. Edit or clear your manually entered city or region at any time. This information is purely text and is never connected to device location hardware.
  • Profile avatar photograph. Tap the avatar in Settings to choose a new photo via the system Photo Picker, or tap Remove photo to permanently delete the image file from internal application storage.

In-app data reset controls

You can perform granular data resets or wipe all application data locally inside Settings:

Study history

Reset learning progress

Permanently clears your recorded question attempts, mastery stages across the 65 objectives, review queues, and custom bookmarks. Curriculum lessons remain unlocked.

Milestones

Reset citizenship journey

Resets your selected preparation milestone and returns the journey roadmap to its initial state.

Residence dates

Reset residence guide

Permanently clears all entered physical presence dates, travel absences, and permanent residency dates from the indicative residence guide.

Full reset

Reset all local data

Wipes all databases, study attempts, journey milestones, residence dates, profile details, and user preferences, returning the app to its fresh install state.

Device and operating system controls

You can also control application settings and permissions through your device operating system:

  • Notifications. You can enable or disable daily study reminders and countdown notifications either inside the app Settings or through Android system notification settings.
  • Appearance and theme. Choose between System Default, Force Light, or Force Dark theme in Settings, and toggle Dynamic Colour integration on compatible Android devices.
  • System data clearance. In Android Settings, open Apps > Citizenship Compass AU > Storage > Clear data to delete all local files and databases via the operating system.
  • Application uninstallation. Removing the application from your phone permanently deletes all local application storage.

Platform availability and future platforms

Platform-specific privacy controls

Android: All controls described on this page are active and accessible in the current Android release.

Future iOS: An iOS version is planned. Equivalent on-device privacy choices and local reset tools will be provided, and platform-specific guidance will be updated prior to the iOS release.
